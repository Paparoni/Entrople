YOUR ROUTE

GUESS BY GUESS

TRAP DETECTOR

SWAP-FAMILY ANALYSIS

Flags any point in your solve where several live candidates agreed on almost the whole word and differed by only one or two letters: the "BATCH / CATCH / HATCH / MATCH / WATCH" situation, where guessing turns into near-blind trial and error.

DEEP MATH

CANDIDATE NARROWING & ENTROPY

For each guess: how many real answers were still possible before and after, the actual bits of information gained, the entropy that guess was expected to yield against the live candidate pool, the shape of the resulting partition, and the strongest guess Entrople could find for that step.
